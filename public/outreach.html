OUTREACH

Helping people see
what’s possible.

We are just beginning to build our team and our community presence. This page reflects where we are starting: small, early, and intentional work that helps students and neighbors get excited about STEM.

We are building this program.

Right now, our outreach work is in its early stages. We are establishing our team, learning how to serve our community best, and preparing to host our first workshops, demos, and mentorship opportunities. This page will grow as we begin those efforts.

PHASE 01

School and community introductions

We are beginning by introducing our team to local students and community groups and sharing what robotics, engineering, and teamwork can look like in practice.

PHASE 02

STEM engagement

As our team grows, we plan to run workshops, answer questions, and help younger students explore design, coding, and problem solving in a hands-on way.

PHASE 03

Mentorship and visibility

We want to create accessible opportunities for students who are curious about robotics, engineering, and what it means to build together as a team.
